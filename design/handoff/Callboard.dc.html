Callboard

PANTONE 2767 C · #14213D

The LinkedIn read, done for film. Three columns on a cool grey ground, 16px cards, pill actions, and the horizontal top nav you liked — Feed, Projects, Classes, Network, Messages, each with its own count. Navy is the whole color story: it carries buttons, active tabs, links and progress, so the interface reads institutional rather than decorative. Community is structural: mutual-connection counts on every person, comment threads open by default, and an Endorse action next to Congratulate. Classes appear four times — nav, a filling-up rail under the composer, an in-progress card with 11 classmates, and a bottom tab on mobile.
